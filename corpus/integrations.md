# Integrations

_Last updated: 2026-01-28_

- **Slack**: get notified about new and escalated tickets in a channel, and reply to
  tickets from Slack. All plans.
- **Shopify**: shows the customer's last 5 orders next to the ticket. Growth and
  Enterprise.
- **Jira**: create a linked Jira issue from a ticket; status changes sync back. Growth
  and Enterprise.
- **Salesforce**: syncs contacts and accounts both ways. Enterprise only.
- **WhatsApp**: connect through a Meta Business account. Requires a plan that includes
  the WhatsApp channel.
