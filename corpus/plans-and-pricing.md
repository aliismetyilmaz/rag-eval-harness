# Plans and Pricing

_Last updated: 2026-01-15_

Relaykit is a customer-support help desk. It is sold per agent seat in three plans.

## Starter

- $19 per agent per month.
- Up to 3 agents.
- Email channel only.
- Ticket search covers the last 30 days of history.
- No API access and no automations.

## Growth

- $49 per agent per month.
- Unlimited agents.
- Email, live chat, and WhatsApp channels.
- Automations (see "Automations and SLAs" for limits).
- Ticket search covers 2 years of history.
- REST API access.

## Enterprise

- Custom pricing; contact sales.
- Everything in Growth, plus SAML single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, an audit log,
  data residency (US or EU), and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Annual billing and trials

- Paying annually gives two months free, which works out to about a 17% discount.
- Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card
  is required to start the trial.
