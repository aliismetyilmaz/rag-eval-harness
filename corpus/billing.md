# Billing

_Last updated: 2026-02-03_

## Seats

Relaykit bills per active agent seat. Adding a seat mid-cycle is prorated daily and
charged immediately. Removing a seat takes effect at the next renewal; Relaykit does not
issue refunds for the remainder of the current billing cycle.

## Payment methods

- Credit and debit cards on all plans.
- ACH bank transfer, for US customers on annual billing only.
- Invoicing with net-30 terms, for Enterprise customers only.

Prices can be paid in USD, EUR, or GBP.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Relaykit retries the charge 3 times over 7 days and emails the
account owner after each attempt. If all retries fail, the account becomes read-only:
agents can view tickets but cannot reply until the balance is paid.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of
the current billing cycle.
