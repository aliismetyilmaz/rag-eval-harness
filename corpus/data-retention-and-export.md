# Data Retention and Export

_Last updated: 2026-03-10_

## Retention

Tickets are kept for as long as the account is active. Deleted tickets go to the trash
and are permanently removed after 30 days.

When an account is cancelled, its data is kept for 60 days so it can be reactivated or
exported, and is then permanently deleted.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers can choose to store their data in the US or the EU. All other plans
are hosted in the US.

## Exporting data

- CSV export is available on all plans.
- JSON export is available on Growth and Enterprise.
- Each export file contains up to 100,000 tickets; larger accounts receive several files.
- CSV exports do not include attachments. JSON exports include attachment download links
  that stay valid for 7 days.

## Privacy requests

Relaykit completes GDPR data deletion requests within 30 days.
